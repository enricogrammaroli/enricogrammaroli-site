Preservation Digital Access
ICPI — Italian Ministry of Culture, Rome · Jan–Jun 2026

Archivio Cirese — digital preservation & access pilot project

Preservation planning and collection management; metadata and finding-aid creation in ArchiveSpace following DACS and ISAD(G) standards; implementation of the public-access portal on Omeka S.

The archive, transferred to ICPI in 2021, brings together the family fondi of folklorist Eugenio Cirese, anthropologist Alberto Mario Cirese, and teacher Aida Ruscitti: over 3,000 units of correspondence, manuscripts, and photographs, 1898–2006.
